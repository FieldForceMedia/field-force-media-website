Privacy Policy

What we collect on this website, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated November 9, 2026

Who we are

Field Force Media is a marketing agency for home service businesses. This policy covers fieldforcemedia.com. Questions go to management@fieldforcemedia.com.

What we collect

  • What you type into the contact form: your name, email, phone, business name, industry, revenue range and message.
  • How the site is used: Google Analytics records pages viewed, rough location (city level), device and browser, and how you found us. It uses cookies, which you can turn off with Decline in the cookie bar.
  • Basic server logs: our host, Cloudflare, briefly processes IP addresses and request details to deliver the site and block attacks.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and talk with you about working together.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services below that help us run the site.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security, and the service that receives the contact form.
  • Resend: delivers contact form submissions to our inbox by email.
  • Google Analytics: site statistics, unless you decline.
  • Google Fonts: loads the site's typefaces, which shares your IP address with Google.

Cookies and your choice

When you first visit, a bar at the bottom of the page lets you accept or decline analytics cookies. Decline and Google Analytics runs without cookies or ad features. Visitors from the EU, UK and Switzerland get no analytics cookies unless they click Accept. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and the bar will appear again.

How long we keep it

Contact form messages stay in our email for as long as we need them to work with you or keep business records. Analytics data is kept under Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. California residents have these rights under the CCPA, and we honor them for everyone. Email management@fieldforcemedia.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is for business owners and isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
