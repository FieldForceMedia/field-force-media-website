Questions.

What makes Field Force Media different from other marketing agencies?
We only work with home service businesses. That's not a niche — it's a total focus. We understand your seasonality, your customer, your margins, and what it actually takes to dominate a local market. No learning curve. No generic playbooks borrowed from e-commerce. Every strategy we build is purpose-built for home service.
What types of home service businesses do you work with?
Lawn care and landscaping, window cleaning, pressure washing, painting, house cleaning, HVAC, pest control, tree service, roofing, pool service, irrigation, gutter cleaning, concrete and paving, fencing, and any other trade that works in and around homes. If homeowners hire you, we can help you grow.
What results can I expect?
Results depend on your starting point, your market, and the services you invest in. Our case study: Island Scape North Shore LLC went from $2,000/month to $35,000/month in under 14 months — a 17.5x revenue increase — using our integrated marketing system. We don't promise that for every client, but we do promise a clear strategy, accountable execution, and full transparency on what's working.
Do you lock clients into long-term contracts?
No. Everything we offer is month to month: cancel any time with 15 days' written notice. We keep clients by delivering results, not by trapping them in contracts. Your ad accounts, pages, Google profile, domain and every lead are in your name, and if you leave you get your finished creative files. Websites come with a monthly care plan (hosting, updates and support); if you leave, you can buy the site outright (landing page $999, full site $1,999, half that after 36 months on a care plan) or keep it on our basic plan.
