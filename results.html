How we got there.

Island Scape North Shore: from $2K to $35K a month

The challenge. A skilled operator stuck at $2,000/month: no website that converted, no ad engine, no brand. A great service business that looked like a side hustle.

What we ran. Website redesign, Meta advertising, brand identity, local SEO, and direct mail, built to work as one system.

"We went from barely covering expenses to $35,000 a month in recurring revenue. For the first time I felt like I had a real business, not just a job." Josh Johnson, Owner

NEO Window Cleaning: 10.7× return on ad spend

The challenge. A newer window cleaning company with capacity to spare and no reliable way to fill the calendar week to week.

What we ran. Meta advertising: offer, creative, and targeting built specifically for local window cleaning demand.

Valley Pest Control: $19K+ in contracts in 9 weeks

The challenge. Strong local reputation, a clear guarantee to sell, and no paid channel turning that into new signed accounts.

What we ran. Meta advertising anchored on the Total Shield guarantee and the same-technician promise, driving to phone and form.

bluEmber Lighting: 1,516 leads in 8 weeks

The challenge. A permanent lighting company that needed a steady stream of homeowner leads to keep its install calendar full.

What we ran. Meta lead ad campaigns for permanent lighting installs, tracked in the client's own ad account.
